# Appendix G — Modern LLM Systems

We've journeyed from the basics of language representation through transformers, training, alignment, and all the supporting technologies.

This final appendix covers the practical and forward-looking aspects:

1. **Efficient Serving**: How to make LLMs practical and affordable
2. **Building Your Own**: Learning by doing, understanding through implementation
3. **The Future**: Where the field is heading

This appendix answers "What do we do with all this knowledge?" and "Where does it go from here?"

The conclusion: LLMs are not a solved problem. The field is rapidly evolving, with new techniques emerging constantly. The fundamentals you've learned—transformers, next-token prediction, attention, scaling—form the foundation. But innovation continues at an accelerating pace.

Understanding these fundamentals means you can grasp new developments as they emerge.
