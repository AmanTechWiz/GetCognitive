# Appendix B — Learning Language Before Transformers

Before transformers revolutionized AI, researchers struggled with a fundamental problem: **how do you train a neural network to understand sequential data like language?**

This appendix covers the pre-transformer era of deep learning. Understanding this history is crucial because it shows why transformers were revolutionary—they solved fundamental problems that had limited earlier architectures for decades.

We'll see how neural networks learn through backpropagation, why feed-forward architectures fail at sequential tasks, and how recurrent architectures like LSTMs attempted to solve these problems. Most importantly, we'll understand their limitations, which directly motivated the transformer breakthrough.
