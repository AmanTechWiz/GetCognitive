# Appendix E — From GPT to ChatGPT

The gap between raw GPT-3 and ChatGPT seems huge, but it comes down to training techniques, not architecture.

This appendix covers how to take a pre-trained language model and transform it into a helpful, conversational assistant:

1. **Fine-tuning**: Adapt the model to new tasks with small amounts of task-specific data
2. **Alignment**: Make the model's behavior match human values
3. **Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback**: Train on human preferences
4. **ChatGPT**: How instruction-following conversational systems actually work

By the end, you'll understand that ChatGPT isn't magic—it's a well-engineered application of training techniques on top of the transformer architecture.
